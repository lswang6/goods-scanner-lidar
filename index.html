Measure boxes with your iPhone

Cargo Measure is a free warehouse and logistics tool for recording inbound cargo. Create an order per customer, walk around each item to get its length, width and height, add photos, and export reports.

Support & FAQPrivacy Policy

  • Walk-around scanning. LiDAR on iPhones that have it; camera-only (about ±3 cm) on others. Boxes and cylinders are detected.
  • Orders and totals. Inbound orders per customer with photos, volume (m³), pieces and weight totals.
  • Reports and export. By date, customer or selected orders; CSV, PDF report and photo ZIP.
  • Private and offline. No account, no server, no analytics, no tracking. All data stays on your device.
  • 11 languages. English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português, Русский, Tiếng Việt.
