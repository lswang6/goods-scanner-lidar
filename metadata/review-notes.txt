Cargo Measure - App Review notes

No login or account is required. The app has no server component and works fully offline; all data stays on the device.

How to test:
1. On first launch a short tutorial is shown. Its last page is an accuracy notice that must be acknowledged with "I understand".
2. Create a customer (Customers) and then an inbound order for that customer (Inbound tab), and add an item to the order.
3. Tap the scan button for the item and walk slowly around any box. iPhones with a LiDAR scanner measure with LiDAR; other iPhones use the camera only. If you cannot scan, you can enter the dimensions (length, width, height) manually instead.
4. Open Reports, choose a date range, customer or selected orders, and export CSV, a PDF report or a photo ZIP. A single order can also be exported from the order itself.
5. Settings contains the calibration offset (Measurement Calibration) and the full accuracy disclaimer (About).

Camera permission is used only for on-device measuring and taking item photos. No data is collected or transmitted.
