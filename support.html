How do I scan an item?

Create a customer and an inbound order, add an item, and tap the scan button. Point your iPhone at the item until the frame locks on, then walk slowly around it until every segment of the ring lights up. Check the result, then save. You can also type in the dimensions manually.

LiDAR or camera: how accurate is it?

iPhones with a LiDAR scanner measure with LiDAR. Other iPhones use the camera only, which is less precise: roughly ±3 cm in our tests. In both cases the results are estimates and accuracy is not guaranteed (see the accuracy disclaimer below).

Tips for good scans
  • Use bright, even lighting.
  • Keep the whole item in view and walk at a steady distance and speed.
  • Place the item on a flat, clear floor or pallet, away from other objects.
  • Very dark, shiny or transparent surfaces and shrink wrap can reduce accuracy.
  • Scan one item at a time; the app records the maximum outer dimensions.
What is the calibration offset?

If you scan a box of known size and every side reads too large or too small, enter the difference in Settings > Measurement Calibration (Offset per Side, cm). For example, if each side reads 1 cm too large, enter 1. Positive values are subtracted.

How do I export?

Open the Reports tab, choose a date range, a customer or specific orders, and export a CSV, a PDF report or a ZIP of photos. You can also export a single order from the order itself.

Where is my data? Is it backed up?

Everything is stored on your device only; there is no account and no server. Depending on your device settings, app data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups. Deleting the app deletes its data, so export anything you need to keep first.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Scanning, orders, reports and exports all work without an internet connection.

Accuracy disclaimer

Measurements are estimates produced from your iPhone's sensors (LiDAR) or camera.

Accuracy is not guaranteed. It varies with the device, the object's shape and material, lighting, surroundings and scanning technique.

Camera measuring, used on iPhones without LiDAR, is less precise: roughly ±3 cm in our tests.

Verify critical dimensions with a tape measure before relying on them, for example for freight charges, packing or customs.
