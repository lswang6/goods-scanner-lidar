Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-03

Summary

Cargo Measure does not collect, transmit to any server, share or sell any personal data. There is no account, and the app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.

Information we do not collect

The developer does not receive any information from the app. Specifically, the app has no sign-up or login, no analytics or crash-reporting service, no advertising, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking.

Camera

The app asks for access to the camera (and, on iPhones that have one, the LiDAR scanner) to measure items and to take photos of them. Camera and sensor data are processed on your device only and are not sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

The orders, customers, measurements, notes and photos you enter are stored locally on your device. If your device backup (for example iCloud or a computer backup) includes app data, this data may be part of that backup under your own settings; the developer has no access to it. Deleting the app deletes its data from your device.

Exports

CSV files, PDF reports and photo ZIP archives are created on your device only when you choose to export. You decide where they go (for example AirDrop, Files or email). Once you share a file with another app or service, that service's own terms and privacy policy apply.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it also collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
